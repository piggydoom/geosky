Geography

Guadeloupe is an island state in the caribbean sea. The small state has a total area of 1,628 km² (629 mi²). This land area is about 1.3 times the size of Los Angeles.

Besides the two main islands, the country has 10 other small islands. Guadeloupe has no direct neighbors. The distance between New York City and the Capital Basse-Terre is about 2,990 km (1,858 mi).

The countrywide time in Guadeloupe is Atlantic Standard Time (AST), which is currently 15 hours behind Sydney. As in many countries, there is no daylight savings time in Guadeloupe. Related to Greenwich Mean Time, there's the same difference of -4 hours all over the year.

Climate in Guadeloupe

In Guadeloupe, as in all equatorial countries, there is a tropical climate. Unlike in Europe or the USA, there is almost no difference between the seasons. The daylight hours vary little, and the temperature differences between summer and winter are also lower. Depending on the season, the average daytime temperatures range between 29 and 32 degrees. In the colder months, the average night temperatures drop down to 22 °C.
